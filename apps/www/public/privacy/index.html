Privacy

shipwords.dev is the website of an open-source project. It has no accounts, no forms and no newsletter, so it never asks for your name or email.

Analytics

To see which pages people read and where they come from, the site loads two analytics tools on shipwords.dev only:

  • Google Analytics 4 sets cookies and sends page views, referrer, device type and approximate location to Google, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Rybbit sets no cookies. It is loaded and sent through shipwords.dev/stats/; your IP address is used to look up the country and city, then the same page-view data is stored.

Neither tool loads when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Blocking cookies or scripts for this site also turns them off; the page works the same.

Sites built with shipwords

Each site built from this code, including tinydeskai.io, is run by its owner and has its own privacy policy.
